Our Architecture Process

A systematic 8-step approach to bringing your architectural vision to life

01

Consultation

Discuss project requirements, budget, and timeline with comprehensive site evaluation

02

Site Analysis

Study topography, climate, environmental factors, and local building regulations

03

Design Development

04

05

Tendering & Procurement

07

Construction Administration

08

Project Completion
